# Official rule verification

Verified against the live official pages on 2026-07-16.

## Eligibility and timing

- Submission period: 2026-06-30 10:00 ET through 2026-08-18 17:00 ET.
- Entrants must be at least 18 or the age of majority in their residence.
- The contest excludes jurisdictions where participation or prize receipt is
  prohibited, including the examples named in the rules.
- Up to five people may enter as a team; this project currently enters individually.

## Pass/fail requirements

- The project must be an agentic application with CockroachDB as persistent memory and
  must be deployed on AWS.
- At least two listed CockroachDB tools must be meaningfully integrated.
- At least one AWS service must meaningfully power the application.
- The repository must be public, open source, licensed, documented, and runnable.
- A functional app URL and a public YouTube/Vimeo demo under three minutes are required.
- The video must show both the functioning project and the CockroachDB memory layer.
- The app must remain freely testable through the judging period.

## New-work boundary

The official rules require a new project created during the submission period. Standard
frameworks, libraries, templates, and AI coding assistants are allowed. Any other
pre-existing code or work must be disclosed, and the submitted work must have been built
during the submission period. Open source is allowed when licenses are followed and the
entry materially builds upon it.

LabRecall is therefore a separate repository and codebase. See
[`PREEXISTING_WORK.md`](PREEXISTING_WORK.md) for the precise disclosure.

## Judging strategy

The five equally weighted criteria are agentic memory design, technical implementation,
real-world impact, product readiness, and creativity/originality. The acceptance gates
in `PROJECT_PLAN.md` map one-to-one to those criteria.
